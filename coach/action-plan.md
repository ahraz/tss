# GTA Scrub — Action Plan

## The Goal
Get the first paying client. Then build to $1M/year.

## Sales Strategy

### Approach A — Plaza Walk-Ins (Saturday)
**Pitch:**
"Hey, I'm Ahraz — I run a commercial cleaning company in Brampton. I was in the area and noticed how busy you guys are. I actually specialize in medical office cleaning with photo-verified reports for compliance. I'm offering a free demo clean — no charge, no contract. Can I leave a card with you or drop one off for the office manager?"

**Goal:** 20+ doors per Saturday

### Approach B — Cold Calls (Wed-Fri 2:30-5pm + Sun-Tue)
**Script:**
"Hi [name], this is Ahraz with GTA Scrub. I clean medical offices in Brampton and offer a free demo clean — no charge, no contract. Are you the person who handles cleaning decisions?"

If yes: "Great. When would work for a free clean so you can see the quality? I also send photo-verified reports so you have proof of what's done."

If not: "Can you share the best email for [decision maker's name]? I'll send a quick intro."

**Goal:** 30 calls/day, split between Ahraz and wife

### Approach C — Facebook Marketplace
Post: "Commercial deep clean — $149 first-time special. Photo-verified proof included. Serving Brampton & GTA."

### Approach D — Facebook Groups
Join: "Brampton Business Owners", "GTA Small Business Network"
Action: Comment on posts, DM businesses that need cleaning

## Additional Channels
### Flyer Drops
- Print 100 flyers (8.5x11, black & white, Staples ~$20)
- Walk plazas at 6-7am before offices open
- Slide under every door
- Offer: "Free demo clean — first 10 businesses"
- Include: phone number, website, QR code

### Google Business Profile Posts
- Post 1x/week minimum
- Before/after photos
- "Free demo clean this month"
- "Serving Brampton medical offices"
- Seasonal offers

### Cold Email
- Export leads with emails from Sheets
- Send: "Hi [name], I clean medical offices in Brampton with photo-verified reports. Free demo clean. No contract."
- 100 emails = 30 min

### Referral Program
- Live on website: gtascrub.com/referrals
- Referrer: $150 gift card or $250 account credit
- New client: $250 off first month
- Tell every prospect, every friend, every family member

### Friends & Family
- Text: "Hey, do you know anyone who owns a business or manages an office in the GTA? I'm looking for my first cleaning client — paying $100 referral fee."
- Most people know someone

## Follow-Up System
- Log every call and walk-in in TSS
- Anyone who didn't say "no" → call back in 1 week
- Repeat up to 5 times
- Track outcomes

## Demo Clean Process (Minimal Viable)
1. Schedule date/time with prospect
2. Show up with equipment and supplies
3. Use TSS to clock in, take before photo
4. Clean the space
5. Complete checklist in TSS
6. Take after photo via TSS clock-out
7. Text before/after photos to prospect immediately
8. Ask: "Want to set up a regular schedule?"
9. If yes: send quote via TSS, start recurring service
10. If yes: activate referral program reward

## Notes / Concerns
- Website claims "500+ clients" and "$2M insurance" — these don't match reality yet. If a prospect spots this and then hears "you'll be my first client," it creates a trust problem. Consider updating the marketing copy.
