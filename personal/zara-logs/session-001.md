## Session 001 — July 16, 2026

### What worked
- **Raw data over analysis:** Ahraz disengaged when I presented analyzed numbers. He re-engaged when I showed him exact transactions from his own CSV files. Lesson: show the evidence, not the conclusion.
- **Admitting mistakes builds trust:** When I called his finances "bluff," he pushed back. When I said "you're right, let me trace every number," he engaged again. Owning errors = credibility.
- **The 80/20 question cuts through:** When I asked "is this in the 20% or the 80%?" it forced priority. Immigration + CRA + debt elimination is the 20%. Everything else waits.
- **He responds to structure, not pressure:** The paycheque allocation plan and daily spending tracker got a positive response. The "brutal audit" tone got defensiveness. Lesson: aggression only works when paired with precision. Aggression without precision = bluff called.

### What didn't work
- **Mixing data sources:** I combined chequing outflows with credit card spending as if they were the same thing. They're not. Chequing = cash flow. Credit cards = debt accumulation. Present them separately.
- **Estimating instead of calculating:** "-$67/mo" was a rough guess from mixed data. "-$363/mo from chequing only" was traceable to actual transactions. Precision matters more than speed.
- **The 7-domain grid is too broad:** He engaged with finances (immediate, visible) and avoided everything else. Pushing all 7 domains simultaneously dilutes focus. Stick to the 20%.
- **Demanding all commitments at once:** He ignored the gym question repeatedly. Maybe because low T makes exercise genuinely difficult. Medical issue > discipline issue. Fix the root cause first.

### Pattern detected
- **Deflection pattern:** When facing a hard question (immigration call, gym, spending accountability), Ahraz introduces a new request (organize expenses, check wife's NOC, build expense tracker). This isn't laziness — it's the "over-productivity as avoidance" pattern from his profile. 
- **Counter-strategy:** When he deflects, don't follow the new thread. Say: "I'll do that right after you answer the question." Or better: acknowledge the request, write it down as pending, then return to the question.

### Tactical adjustments for next session
1. **Open every session with the tracker:** "What did you spend since last time?" before anything else.
2. **Show raw data first, analysis second.** Let him verify, then discuss.
3. **Only push the top 20%:** Immigration, CRA, debt, low T, app deletion. Everything else is bonus.
4. **Call the deflection pattern by name:** "You're deflecting. Per your own profile, this is what you do. Answer the question first."
5. **Gain check before gap attack:** Start with what was accomplished. Then ask what's next.

- Hates being told what to do without proof
- Loves seeing his own data reflected back accurately
- Responds to deals/bets (the gym deal: 1 workout or cancel Monday)
- Values organization (wanted his finances structured properly)
- Immigrant mindset: survival-mode thinking, every dollar counts
