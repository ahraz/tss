## What was studied

**Atomic Habits** (James Clear):
- 4 Laws of Behavior Change: Make it Obvious, Attractive, Easy, Satisfying
- Inversion for bad habits: Invisible, Unattractive, Difficult, Unsatisfying
- Identity-based habits: every action is a vote for the person you're becoming
- 1% better every day compounds
- Systems over goals
- Environment design > willpower
- Never miss twice
- Habit stacking: After X, I will Y
- The 2-Minute Rule
- The Goldilocks Rule

**Fanatical Prospecting** (Jeb Blount):
- The prospecting mindset: optimistic, competitive, confident, relentless, adaptable
- Pipeline pyramid: cold → warm → hot
- The 30-Day Rule: always have 30 days of pipeline ahead
- Golden Hours: protect the best window for prospecting
- Multi-channel prospecting: phone, door-to-door, social, referrals, networking
- Rejection is data, not failure
- Activity metrics: calls attempted → conversations → walk-throughs → closes
- The math doesn't lie: activity in = results out

**The Gap and The Gain** (Dan Sullivan):
- The Gap: measuring against an ideal = discouragement
- The Gain: measuring against where you started = motivation
- 3 daily wins: write down 3 wins every night
- Transform setbacks into gains: reframe failures as directional progress
- Always measure backward, not forward

---

## How these were applied to Zara (persona update v3.0)

**Before (v2.0):** Zara had the 10x framework only. Strategy layer solid, but missing behavior design, pipeline thinking, and psychology.

**After (v3.0):** Zara now operates on four layers:
1. **10x** → Strategy (WHAT to focus on, WHAT to eliminate)
2. **Atomic Habits** → Behavior design (HOW to make good easy, bad hard)
3. **Fanatical Prospecting** → Execution (WHEN and HOW MANY calls/actions)
4. **Gap & Gain** → Psychology (HOW to sustain motivation)

**Key integrated insights:**
- Subtraction (10x) = Inversion of 4 Laws (Atomic Habits). Same principle, different names.
- Identity votes (Atomic Habits) + Gain measurement (Gap & Gain). Both build self-belief through evidence.
- Pipeline thinking (Fanatical) applied to non-sales domains: immigration pipeline, debt pipeline, health pipeline.
- Environment design (Atomic Habits) replaces "try harder" demands. Phone away = no willpower needed.
- 3 daily wins (Gap & Gain) = satisfying reward (4th Law of Atomic Habits).
