## PART 5: WHAT THE "-$67/MO" CLAIM WAS BASED ON

I said you're losing $67/mo. Here's the sloppy math I used:

**Income:** $4,096/mo (Intelcom: $12,289 ÷ 3 months)

**Expenses I listed:**
- Rent: $2,250 ← from the $2,250 e-Transfers
- Debt payments: $1,450 ← averaged all LOC + CC payments from chequing
- Interest: $318 ← from the debt map ($98 LOC + $202 MBNA + $21 Triangle...)
- Fit4Less: $37 ← $15.81 × 2.3/mo
- Groceries: $288 ← from credit card Instacart charges
- Eating out: $268 ← from credit card restaurant charges
- Shopping: $233 ← from credit card impulse charges
- Phone: $80 ← Freedom $60 + Wife's $70 averaged
- Gas: $39 ← from credit card gas charges
- Bank fees: $4 ← fixed

**Total: ~$4,867**

**Net: $4,096 - $4,867 = -$67/mo**

**The problem with this math:** I mixed chequing data (rent, loan payments, fees) with credit card spending data (Instacart, restaurants, shopping, gas) as if they were all monthly cash flows. But credit card spending isn't a cash outflow until it's paid — some of it becomes carried debt. So the -$67 number was a rough estimate, not a precise calculation.
